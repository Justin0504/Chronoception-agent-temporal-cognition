\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We argued that LLM agents do not perceive their own time, and measured the failure on 10 agent configurations from 3 providers and $\sim$4{,}000 trajectories. The argument has three layers. \textbf{Theoretical:} CIT (Theorem~\ref{thm:cit}) establishes that token-only loss carries no gradient signal aligning $\twall$ with any internal representation; the Reverse-Scaling Theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:reverse-scaling}) establishes that reasoning-token expansion strictly degrades narrative-axis chronoception. \textbf{Empirical:} every panel agent uses $\leq 5\%$ of wall-clock budgets (L2); retrospective $|\confab|$ closes along the non-reasoning capability axis but grows along the reasoning-token axis ($1.23\to1.67$ across o4-mini efforts; $0.07 \to 0.16$ with extended thinking, sign-flipping); nominally 90\% intervals on self-duration achieve $0$--$50\%$ coverage; no agent crosses $\ccestar = 0.20$. \textbf{Engineering:} consumer web-chat products install wall-clock injection with $100\%$ universality across competing labs --- non-experimental evidence that the underlying models lack a representation of time.

The framework's bridge to deployment is the Agentic Timeline Hypothesis: $T_{\max} \propto 1/\cce$, with reasoning compute making the bound strictly tighter. Pre-registered P12 is qualitatively supported by 14{,}709 public METR HCAST runs (reasoning models decay $20\%$ faster at matched short-horizon success). The framework generalises along its other axis: the Cartographic Problem, SIT (Theorem~\ref{thm:sit}), and the Agentic Frontier $T_{\max}\cdot S_{\max} \leq C/\ccestST$ extend the bound to the joint $(T, S)$ plane (\S\ref{sec:future-work}, Appendix~\ref{app:spatiotemporal}) --- the bridge to a planned Paper 3 on long-horizon agent benchmarks.

We release \textsc{ChronoBench}, the Injection Atlas, all $\sim$4{,}000 trajectories, the Docker reproducibility package, a locked OSF pre-registration, and a \texttt{pip}-installable evaluation package. The Augustine threshold is uncrossable through narrative training; reasoning-token expansion makes it harder; the spatial axis predicts the same structure. Closing it is the program of \textsc{ChronoStack} (Paper 2) and \textsc{ChronoStack}$^{+}$ (Paper 3).
