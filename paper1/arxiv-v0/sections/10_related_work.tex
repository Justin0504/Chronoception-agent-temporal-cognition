\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\textbf{Concurrent work on agent temporal awareness.} Three papers operate in the same neighbourhood; each touches one axis we unify. \citet{garikaparthi2026canllmstime} measures duration self-reports on four non-reasoning model families; we extend to reasoning models (the Hidden-Time sign flip, §\ref{ssec:reverse-scaling}), prove the Reverse-Scaling Theorem, and confirm it cross-vendor on Sonnet 4.6 thinking. \citet{ma2026timelymachine} (Timely Machine) installs the Parkinson regime via budget-aware reward shaping; our finding that native $\parkinson \approx 0$ across the panel is the contrapositive --- the regime they construct does not exist without budget-aware training. \citet{cheng2025temporallyblind} documents agent failures in honouring time-bounded sub-tasks; we add the formal axis split, CIT, the Calibration Catastrophe, the Injection Atlas, and the Agentic Timeline.

\textbf{Foundational lineage.} Augustine's \emph{Confessions} XI \citep{augustine_confessions} names the central problem; cognitive-science chronoception \citep{wittmann2009chronoception,eagleman2008time} supplies the operational definition and the calibration of $\ccestar$; \citet{parkinson1955} supplies L1.

\textbf{Distinguished from temporal reasoning.} \citet{chu2024timebench}'s TimeBench measures reasoning over external timelines; \citet{goel2025chronocept} measures temporal validity of facts. Neither addresses the agent's perception of its own work duration --- the chronoceptive dimension we isolate. An agent can perform well on TimeBench and yet exhibit catastrophic Augustine Problem.

\textbf{Inverse-scaling and calibration literature.} \citet{inversescalingttc2025} documents inverse scaling in test-time compute on calibration and confidence tasks; the Reverse-Scaling Theorem is the structural analogue for chronoception specifically, with the additional theoretical content that the degradation is monotone under CIT. Modern calibration tooling \citep{guo2017calibration,desai2020calibration,jiang2021calibration,kadavath2022knowing,dontthinktwice2025} closes token-level confidence gaps to a few percentage points, but none applies to wall-clock duration calibration: the relevant signal is not in the loss support. The Calibration Catastrophe (\S\ref{ssec:calibration}) is the direct empirical projection of CIT on the duration sub-problem.

\textbf{Agent benchmarks.} \citep{agentbench,swebench,webarena,gaia,kwa2025metrhorizons} measure aggregate task success across many capabilities. Our benchmark is diagnostic: it isolates chronoception. The two are complementary --- aggregate benchmarks reveal that chronoceptive failures correlate with long-horizon failure (\citet{kwa2025metrhorizons}'s HCAST scaling curve, used as P12 evidence in \S\ref{sec:agentic-timeline}); diagnostic benchmarks supply the mechanism.
