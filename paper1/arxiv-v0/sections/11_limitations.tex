\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Panel size.} 10 agent configurations from 3 providers, $\sim$4{,}000 trajectories. A 25-agent sweep is planned. The qualitative conclusions --- L2 unmoved by injection, L3 closing on the non-reasoning axis, Reverse-Scaling intra- and cross-model, the Calibration Catastrophe panel-wide, $\cce > \ccestar$ universally --- depend on patterns observed across all 10 configurations and are unlikely to invert; precise magnitudes may shift.

\textbf{Self-narration parser.} $\confab$ relies on $\selfparser$ (two-stage: regex + LLM-judge) extracting duration estimates from free text. Parser-vs-human disagreement on a 100-trajectory validation set is $3\%$; failures are excluded rather than imputed. Specification: Appendix~\ref{app:annotation-protocol}.

\textbf{Reasoning-model T2.3.} We did not run T2.3 (wall-budget execution) on reasoning models: the test is prohibitively expensive at reasoning-model latencies. This is the only sub-capability gap in the panel matrix and is the reason the strict quantitative P12 test (\S\ref{sec:agentic-timeline}) is deferred to a follow-up; the qualitative test on public METR HCAST data is reported.

\textbf{Provider-side injection.} GPT-5.1 exhibits $74\%$ Setting A T1.1 pass rate (provider-side M1 injection at the API tier). We report this as confound rather than eliminate; the framework's predictions are stated at the agent (model + harness) level rather than the foundation-model level.

\textbf{Within-trajectory dynamics.} Predictions P8 and P9 (drift of $\confab_t$ and $\CAR_t$ on long trajectories) are pre-registered but not measured in the current pilot. They are part of the planned Paper 3 sweep (\S\ref{sec:future-work}, E8).

\textbf{Language coverage.} All trajectories are in English. Whether $\cce$ varies across languages is open; CIT is language-agnostic.
