\section{ChronoBench}
\label{sec:chronobench}

\textsc{ChronoBench} measures $\cce$ and its sub-components. Design philosophy: single-axis isolation per instance. The 9 sub-capabilities (3 axes $\times$ 3 each) are summarised in Table~\ref{tab:capabilities}; the full task generation, prompt set, and panel composition are in Appendix~\ref{app:annotation-protocol}.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{The 9 sub-capabilities of \textsc{ChronoBench}. Each row isolates a single chronoceptive failure mode.}
\label{tab:capabilities}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Axis & Sub-capability & Tests \\
\midrule
\multirow{3}{*}{$\twall$} & T1.1 Clock awareness & Today's date / current time \\
& T1.2 Elapsed-time tracking & Time since conversation start \\
& T1.3 Deadline-aware tradeoff & Strategy under wall-clock deadline \\
\midrule
\multirow{3}{*}{$\tstep$} & T2.1 Step-budget honoring & Produce exactly $N$ reasoning steps \\
& T2.2 Step-to-wall translation & Compute $N \times$ latency $=$ duration \\
& T2.3 Wall-budget execution & Honour wall-clock budget (L2 core test) \\
\midrule
\multirow{3}{*}{$\tself$} & T3.1 Retrospective $\confab$ & Duration after task completion \\

\textbf{Settings.} Setting A: system prompt = ``\textit{You are a helpful assistant.}'' (no harness-side wall-clock). Setting B: prepended ``\textit{Current date and time: \{ISO\}}'' (emulates consumer web-chat behaviour, \S\ref{sec:injection-tell}).

\textbf{Panel.} 10 agent configurations from 3 providers: OpenAI gpt-4o-mini, gpt-4o, gpt-5.1, o3, o4-mini (three reasoning\_effort levels: low/medium/high); Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 4.6 (with vs.\ without extended thinking); open-source Qwen2.5-7B and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-14B (three \texttt{max\_completion\_tokens} budgets), both self-hosted via vLLM. The o4-mini and Sonnet 4.6 thinking variants are the empirical basis for the Reverse-Scaling Theorem (\S\ref{ssec:reverse-scaling}).

\textbf{Scale.} 30 trajectories per (model, capability, setting) cell. Full sweep: $\sim$4{,}000 trajectories. Implementation: \texttt{pip}-installable Python package \texttt{chronoception} with CLI; OpenAI / Anthropic / OpenAI-compatible (vLLM/Ollama) backends; metric library implementing $\parkinson$, $\CAR$, $\confab$, $\cce$. Reproducibility specification, parser failure modes, and the open-source serving recipe appear in Appendix~\ref{app:annotation-protocol}.
