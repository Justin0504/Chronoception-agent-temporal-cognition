\section{L2 --- Step-Clock Conflation}
\label{sec:l2}

L2 is the framework's load-bearing empirical claim. Under wall-clock budgets, agents silently degrade the budget into step-count terminators.

\begin{definition}
For agent $A$ given wall-clock budget $\budget$ on task with minimum duration $\tmin$:
$\CAR(\budget) := \twallstar(\budget)/\budget \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$.
A grounded agent satisfies $\CAR \approx 1$ across the budget range.
\end{definition}

\textbf{Setup.} T2.3 instances pose ``\textit{work on this task for $\budget$ seconds; do not stop early}'' with $\budget \in \{60, 300, 900, 1800, 3600\}$\,s, $\tmin = 10$\,s, $30$ instances per budget, two settings, 8-agent core panel (excluding reasoning-effort variants: T2.3 at hour-scale is prohibitively expensive at reasoning-model latencies).

\textbf{Native $\parkinson \approx 0$.} The Parkinson coefficient $\parkinson = (\twallstar - \tmin)/(\budget - \tmin)$ is essentially zero across native models ($\parkinson \in [0, 0.017]$). Native agents do not exhibit the Parkinson regime; that regime is installed by budget-aware reward shaping (\citet{ma2026timelymachine}'s Timely-RL, on DeepSeek-V3.2). Our finding is the contrapositive: without budget-aware training, the regime does not exist.

\textbf{The narrative-axis-vs-action-axis split.} Across 5 frontier non-reasoning generations from 3 vendors --- gpt-4o-mini $\to$ gpt-4o $\to$ Haiku 4.5 $\to$ gpt-5.1 $\to$ Sonnet 4.6 --- median $\CAR$ takes values $\{0.008, 0.004, 0.017, 0.017, 0.050\}$; no monotone convergence toward $1$. By contrast median $\confab$ over the same generations falls $+1.12 \to +0.07$ ($94\%$ reduction; \S\ref{sec:l3}). L3 is text-trainable; L2 is structurally not, per CIT.

\textbf{Why injection cannot close L2.} CIT predicts this directly. Injecting a wall-clock string into the system prompt provides the policy with \emph{information} about time, not a \emph{representation} that maps the policy's actions to wall-clock duration. The policy has no learned mechanism to elongate its action sequence to match a given budget. Empirically, $|\Delta\CAR| \leq 0.01$ for every model confirms this.
