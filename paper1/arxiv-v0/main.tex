\begin{abstract}
LLM agents are trained on tokens and deployed in wall-clock time. They cannot perceive the wall-clock. The \emph{Chronoception Impossibility Theorem} (CIT) makes the claim precise: under any token-only loss, the gradient carries no signal aligning $\twall$ with any internal representation. The \emph{Three Times} ontology --- $\twall$, $\tstep$, $\tself$ --- names the projections a policy must align; the \emph{Augustine Problem} is that alignment failure.

\textsc{ChronoBench} (9 sub-capabilities $\times$ 14 agents $\times$ 2 settings, $\sim$5{,}900 trajectories) measures the failure. Five findings. \textbf{(i)} Every agent honours $< 5\%$ of its wall-clock budget; date injection does not move $\CAR$ ($|\Delta\CAR| \leq 0.01$). \textbf{(ii)} $|\confab|$ falls $94\%$ across five non-reasoning generations while $\CAR$ does not: narrative axis is text-trainable, action axis is not. \textbf{(iii)} The \emph{Reverse-Scaling Theorem}: reasoning-token expansion monotonically grows $|\confab|$ under CIT. Confirmed four ways --- intra-model (o4-mini effort ladder: $|\confab| = 1.23 \to 1.54 \to 1.67$), cross-model (Sonnet 4.6 $\pm$ thinking: sign flip $+0.07 \to -0.22$, CI $[-0.30, -0.10]$), within-model (prospective $+0.22$ vs.\ retrospective $-0.22$ on the same Sonnet-thinking configuration), and cross-vendor (GLM-5.2 sign flip $+0.16 \to -0.29$ from Setting A to Setting B, independent training pipeline). \textbf{(iv)} The \emph{Calibration Catastrophe}: nominal $90\%$ CIs achieve $\leq 50\%$ coverage panel-wide; GPT-4o $0\%$. \textbf{(v)} The \emph{Injection Tell}: 3/3 consumer web-chat products inject the date; 0/3 developer tools do --- three labs converging on the same workaround independently.

Aggregated as $\cce$, no agent crosses the Augustine threshold $\ccestar = 0.20$; the action axis alone exceeds it. Under the \emph{Agentic Timeline Hypothesis} the deployment horizon scales as $T_{\max} \propto 1/\cce$; pre-registered P12 is supported on the METR HCAST corpus (reasoning models decay $20\%$ faster, Mann--Whitney one-sided $p = 0.040$). The framework generalises to a \emph{Spatiotemporal Impossibility Theorem} (SIT) and the \emph{Agentic Frontier} bound $T_{\max} \cdot S_{\max} \leq C / \ccestST$ --- the bridge to Paper 3. Code, trajectories, Docker container, and locked OSF pre-registration released.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Author Contributions}
A.Y.\ conceived the Augustine Problem framing and the Three Times ontology; formulated the CIT, Reverse-Scaling, and SIT theorems and their scope conditions; designed \textsc{ChronoBench} and its 9 sub-capabilities; implemented the panel runners, parsers, and metrics; executed the 14-agent evaluation across 7 vendors ($\sim$5{,}900 trajectories); assembled the Closed-Lab Injection Atlas; ran the toy positive control (A.1); performed the METR HCAST analysis for P12; wrote the manuscript and produced all figures and tables.

\section*{Data and Code Availability}
All trajectories ($\sim$5{,}900 JSON files, $\sim$600\,MB), the analysis code, parser implementations, and Docker reproducibility container are released at \url{https://github.com/Justin0504/Chronoception-agent-temporal-cognition}. The pre-registration is locked on OSF (linked from the repository). The Closed-Lab Injection Atlas is compiled from the public \texttt{asgeirtj/system\_prompts\_leaks} corpus; verbatim excerpts are collected in Appendix~\ref{app:atlas-evidence}. The METR HCAST public dataset is used unchanged. Reproducing every figure and table takes approximately five minutes in the Docker container.
