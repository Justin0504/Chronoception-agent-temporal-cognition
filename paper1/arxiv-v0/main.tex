\begin{abstract}
Large language model agents are trained on token sequences and deployed in wall-clock time. They cannot perceive the wall-clock, and we prove this perception cannot be acquired from any token-only training loss. The Chronoception Impossibility Theorem (CIT) makes the claim precise: the gradient of any loss whose support lies entirely in token sequences carries no signal aligning external wall-clock time with any internal representation. The Three Times ontology --- $\twall$ (external), $\tstep$ (policy invocations), $\tself$ (the agent's narration) --- names the projections an agent's policy must align; the \emph{Augustine Problem} is the failure of that alignment. \textsc{ChronoBench}, a 9-capability diagnostic suite, measures the failure on 10 agent configurations across 3 providers ($\sim$5{,}200 trajectories; the Sonnet 4.6 $+$ extended-thinking variant carries the first full $9\!\times\!2\!\times\!30$ panel coverage of any reasoning model).

Five findings emerge. \textbf{(i)} Every panel agent honors less than $5\%$ of the wall-clock budget given to it (L2 Step-Clock Conflation), and date injection does not move the number ($|\Delta\CAR| \leq 0.01$). \textbf{(ii)} Self-narration error $|\confab|$ closes monotonically across five non-reasoning frontier generations ($+1.12 \to +0.07$, a $94\%$ reduction) while $\CAR$ does not change: the narrative axis is text-trainable; the action axis is not. \textbf{(iii)} The \emph{Reverse-Scaling Theorem} (Theorem~\ref{thm:reverse-scaling}): under CIT, reasoning-token expansion monotonically grows $|\confab|$ within a fixed agent. We confirm it intra-model (o4-mini at three reasoning-effort levels: $|\confab|$ goes $1.23 \to 1.54 \to 1.67$) and cross-model (Claude Sonnet 4.6 with vs.\ without extended thinking, Setting B $n=15$: median $\rho$ shifts from $+0.07$ to $-0.22$ with 95\% bootstrap CI $[-0.30, -0.10]$, a sign flip; on Setting A $n=9$ the same shift point-estimates at $-0.16$ but the CI brackets zero, so the cross-model claim rests primarily on Setting B and on the intra-model effort sweep). Within the same Sonnet-thinking configuration, the sign flip appears again between prospective self-report ($\rho = +0.22$ on T3.2, $n=30$) and retrospective self-report ($\rho = -0.22$ on T3.1, $n=15$) --- a direct within-model prospective/retrospective asymmetry that Theorem~\ref{thm:reverse-scaling} predicts. \textbf{(iv)} The \emph{Calibration Catastrophe}: a nominally $90\%$ confidence interval on the agent's own work-duration achieves at most $50\%$ actual coverage across the panel; GPT-4o achieves $0\%$. \textbf{(v)} The \emph{Injection Tell}: every consumer web-chat product we audited injects today's date into its system prompt (3/3, verbatim leaked-prompt evidence); no developer-tool product does (0/3). Competing labs converge on the same workaround independently --- a natural experiment that the underlying models lack a representation of time.

We argue these are not five surprises but one consequence. Aggregated as a single scalar $\cce$, the failures place every panel agent above the Augustine threshold $\ccestar = 0.20$ (calibrated at $2\times$ human introspective error). The action-axis component alone exceeds the threshold for every agent: narrative-axis training cannot close the gap, and reasoning-token expansion widens it. The Agentic Timeline Hypothesis turns the measurement into a deployment bound, $T_{\max}(A) \propto 1/\cce(A)$. Pre-registered prediction P12 is qualitatively supported by 14{,}709 public METR HCAST runs: reasoning models decay $20\%$ faster than non-reasoning models at matched short-horizon success. The framework generalises along its natural other axis: a Spatiotemporal Impossibility Theorem (SIT, Theorem~\ref{thm:sit}), the \emph{Cartographic Problem}, and the \emph{Agentic Frontier} bound $T_{\max} \cdot S_{\max} \leq C / \ccestST$ extend the claim to the joint $(T, S)$ plane on which long-horizon agents are deployed --- the bridge to a planned third paper. We release \textsc{ChronoBench}, the Injection Atlas, all $\sim$5{,}200 trajectories, a Docker reproducibility package, and a locked OSF pre-registration.
\end{abstract}
