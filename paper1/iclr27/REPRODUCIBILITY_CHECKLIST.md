# ICLR 2027 Reproducibility Checklist — Filled Answers

## Claims and rationale

1. **Are the paper's main claims stated in the abstract and introduction?**
   Yes. Abstract enumerates five findings and the two theorems. §1 states the Position, five contributions, and the Falsification suite.

2. **Are the assumptions of every theoretical result stated?**
   Yes. Assumption (token-only loss support) §3 precedes CIT (Theorem 1). Assumption (budget-honoured reasoning) §6.1 precedes Reverse-Scaling (Theorem 2). Assumption (token-only spatial support) precedes SIT (Theorem 3, appendix E). Scope remarks explicitly out-of-scope items.

3. **Does the paper include complete proofs?**
   Proof sketches in main text (all three theorems, Proposition 1). Full arguments in Appendix. The proofs are elementary (gradient invariance under time reparametrisation); no long derivation is elided.

## Datasets and models

4. **Does the paper report the dataset(s) used?**
   Yes. ChronoBench trajectories: 5,900 JSONs across 14 agents × 9 sub-capabilities × 2 settings. METR HCAST public dataset (24,008 runs analyzed for P12). All released.

5. **Any human-subjects data?**
   No. All measurements are on LLM agent trajectories generated by the authors; no human subjects, no PII.

6. **Are the specific models used identified with version strings?**
   Yes. Explicit versions in §4: OpenAI gpt-4o-mini/gpt-4o/gpt-5.1/o3/o4-mini (three reasoning-effort levels); Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 4.6 (± extended thinking, 8k budget); open-source Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B (three max_completion_tokens); GLM-5.2-FP8, MiniMax-M2.7, Kimi-K2.6, Qwen3.6-27B via Vultr Inference.

## Experimental setup

7. **Are experimental details described (hardware, training config, hyperparameters)?**
   Yes. §4 (settings), §5 (T2.3 budgets and n per cell), §6.3 (A.1 LoRA: rank 16, three epochs, 500 SFT pairs, ±15% Gaussian noise on duration targets, base Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B). All numerical hyperparameters in the paper or Appendix A.

8. **Are the number of runs and statistical significance reported?**
   Yes. 30 trajectories per (model, capability, setting) cell. P12 uses Mann-Whitney U (p=0.040 one-sided), permutation test (p=0.037), rank-biserial r=-0.55. Bootstrap 95% CIs on per-cell rho (10,000 resamples) in Appendix B. Wilson intervals on T1.1 pass rates.

9. **Are compute requirements reported?**
   Yes. Panel evaluation: OpenAI + Anthropic API calls (~$200 total). Vultr Inference for Chinese-lab expansion (~$5 total, 5 API keys × ~100 credits each). Open-source models self-hosted on 8×RTX 6000 Ada. A.1 LoRA fine-tune: ~60 seconds per scale on single RTX 6000 Ada. Docker container reproduces every figure and table in ~5 minutes.

## Code and data release

10. **Is code released?**
    Yes. Full source at https://github.com/Justin0504/Chronoception-agent-temporal-cognition, including pipeline scripts, parsers, metric computations, figure generators, and the P12 U-test.

11. **Is data released?**
    Yes. All 5,900 trajectory JSONs, per-cell parser outputs, and the metrics_with_ci.csv are in the repository (~600 MB uncompressed).

12. **Is the Docker container release available?**
    Yes. `docker pull ...` reproduces every figure and table in ~5 minutes from committed data.

## Pre-registration

13. **Are any predictions pre-registered?**
    Yes. OSF pre-registration is locked (referenced as `osf-prereg-2026` in the bibliography). Pre-registered predictions P1–P13 are enumerated; F1–F4 falsification conditions are stated in §10 of the paper. The pre-registration was locked before the panel expansion (Route B-1) and before the P12 quantitative analysis; results are reported as pre-registered.

## Ethics and broader impact

14. **Ethical review?**
    Not required. No human subjects; no PII; benchmark trajectories are on prompts we authored ourselves or that the models refuse to answer.

15. **Foreseeable harms?**
    Position paper documents a limitation of deployed LLM agents. Publishing may inform practitioners about deployment horizon bounds; no direct harm identified.

16. **Environmental impact?**
    Total inference: ~5,900 traj × mean 15-20s wall-clock = ~30 GPU-hours of API-time equivalent. A.1 LoRA fine-tune: ~1 minute of GPU time per scale. Total estimated CO2 negligible (<10 kg).
