LLM agents are trained on tokens and deployed in wall-clock time. They cannot perceive the wall-clock, and we prove this perception cannot be acquired from any token-only training loss. The Chronoception Impossibility Theorem (CIT) makes the claim precise; the Three Times ontology (τ_wall, τ_step, τ_self) names the projections a policy must align; the Augustine Problem is that alignment failure. On ChronoBench (9 sub-capabilities × 14 agents from 7 vendors × 2 settings, ~5,900 trajectories, including the first full 9×2×30 panel of a reasoning model): every agent leaves ≥89% of its wall-clock budget unspent (panel median CAR ≤ 0.104; non-reasoning median 0.014), and date injection does not move CAR (|ΔCAR| ≤ 0.01). The Reverse-Scaling Theorem, a direct corollary of CIT for budget-honoured reasoning budget K, states that E[|ρ| given K] is monotone non-decreasing in K; we confirm it four ways — intra-model (o4-mini effort ladder, 1.23 → 1.54 → 1.67), cross-model (Sonnet 4.6 ± extended thinking, sign flip +0.07 → −0.17), within-model (prospective +0.16 vs. retrospective −0.25 on the same tasks), and cross-vendor (GLM-5.2 sign flip +0.16 → −0.29, independent training pipeline). The Calibration Catastrophe follows: nominal 90% confidence intervals achieve 0–77% coverage panel-wide. The Injection Tell is a cross-vendor natural experiment: 3/3 consumer web-chat products inject the current date and 0/3 developer tools do, three closed labs converging on the same workaround independently. A toy positive control (Qwen2.5-1.5B with LoRA SFT on wall-clock-supported targets) crosses the Augustine threshold ε* = 0.20 on retrospective duration after ~60 s of training — the constructive converse of CIT. The Agentic Timeline Hypothesis, T_max ∝ 1/ε, is quantitatively supported on METR HCAST (14,709 runs; Mann-Whitney one-sided p = 0.040; permutation p = 0.037). Code, trajectories, and a locked pre-registration are released.
